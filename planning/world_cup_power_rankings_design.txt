World Cup Power Rankings Design

Purpose:
Create a final Gold layer ranking that combines team strength and player impact into one business-ready score. This dataset will act as the main ranking system for the World Cup Intelligence Platform.

Input Datasets:
- data/gold/team_strength_index/team_strength_index.json
- data/gold/player_dependency_score/player_dependency_score.json

Main Idea:
A strong team should rank highly if it has:
- strong team performance
- high win percentage
- strong points per match
- good goal difference
- productive players
- highly rated player contributors

Output Dataset:
data/gold/world_cup_power_rankings/world_cup_power_rankings.json

Key Input Fields From Team Strength Index:
- team_id
- team_name
- league_name
- season
- rank
- points
- matches_played
- wins
- losses
- goals_for
- goals_against
- goals_diff
- win_percentage
- points_per_match
- team_strength_score
- strength_rank

Key Input Fields From Player Dependency Score:
- team_id
- player_name
- position
- rating
- goal_contributions
- goals_per_90
- assists_per_90
- player_dependency_score
- dependency_rank

Derived Team Player Metrics:
For each team, calculate:
- average_player_dependency_score
- top_player_dependency_score
- top_player_name
- total_goal_contributions

Formula Version 1:
World Cup Power Score =
(team_strength_score * 0.70)
+
(average_player_dependency_score * 0.20)
+
(top_player_dependency_score * 0.10)

Reasoning:
- Team strength should matter most because football is team-based.
- Average player dependency shows overall squad quality.
- Top player dependency captures whether a team has a star player who can heavily influence results.

Output Fields:
- team_id
- team_name
- league_name
- season
- strength_rank
- team_strength_score
- average_player_dependency_score
- top_player_dependency_score
- top_player_name
- total_goal_contributions
- world_cup_power_score
- power_rank

Business Value:
This ranking creates a simple, explainable way to compare teams using both team-level and player-level performance. It can support dashboard storytelling, matchup previews, team comparisons, and future match prediction logic.

Notes:
This is Version 1 of the formula. It is not intended to be a perfect prediction model. The purpose is to show how multiple Gold datasets can be combined into a final analytics-ready output.
