Silver Layer Purpose:
Combine and standardize bronze datasets into analytics-ready tables.

Bronze inputs:
- teams_bronze.json
- fixtures_bronze.json
- standings_bronze.json
- players_bronze.json

Silver goals:
- connect teams to fixtures
- connect teams to standings
- connect players to teams
- standardize names and IDs
- prepare data for team strength and match analysis

First Silver Table:
team_performance_silver.json

Purpose:
Create an analytics-ready team performance table by combining team identity data with standings/performance data.

Inputs:
- data/bronze/teams/teams_bronze.json
- data/bronze/standings/standings_bronze.json

Output:
- data/silver/team_performance/team_performance_silver.json

Join Logic:
Join teams_bronze and standings_bronze using team_id.

Key Fields:
- team_id
- team_name
- country
- league_name
- season
- rank
- points
- matches_played
- wins
- draws
- losses
- goals_for
- goals_against
- goals_diff
- form

Why This Table Matters:
This table becomes the foundation for team-level analytics such as team strength, form comparison, ranking analysis, and future match prediction features.

Derived Metrics

win_percentage
Formula:
wins / matches_played * 100

Purpose:
Measure overall team win rate.

----------------------------------

points_per_match
Formula:
points / matches_played

Purpose:
Measure how efficiently a team earns points.
